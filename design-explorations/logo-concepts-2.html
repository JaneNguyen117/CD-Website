//Brand exploration · Round 2 · Harbor

Three marks tuned for "anchored."

Round 1 read "Dock" as a mechanical connector. These three start from the corrected interpretation — Dock as harbor mooring: stable, sturdy, reliable, trustworthy.

brief // Heavier visual weight, grounded composition, solid filled forms. Each mark should feel like it would not move if you pushed it.
Concept 04 Plinth

Plinth

Three stacked masonry tiers — widest and dimmest at the base, narrowest and brightest at the top. Reads as a stepped pyramid, a temple plinth, a podium. Distinct from "Stack" because the proportions are chunkier and architectural rather than signal-bar thin. The mark is the dock; the bright top tier is what rests securely on it.

masonry / monument / founded

Concept 05 Mooring

Mooring

A pier viewed in elevation: a heavy horizontal deck supported by three short pilings driven downward, with a bollard / mooring point glowing above. Most directly harbor-themed of the three — the visual literally is a dock. The accent dot is the mooring eye where the signal ties up.

pier / bollard / tied off

Concept 06 Keystone

Keystone

A single solid cut-stone silhouette — a hexagonal block oriented with flat top and bottom edges. Reads as one shaped piece of masonry: cut to fit, locked in place, immovable. Most abstract of the three but the strongest semantic claim on "trustworthy / load-bearing." The accent embed is the centerpoint that locks the form.

cut stone / load-bearing / fitted
